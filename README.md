One image that sums up Wednesday 18 November 2026: a big date header plus
six panels, each with a flat icon drawn from scratch, a short headline and
one factual sub-line.

The six stories: Iran strikes Kuwait and Bahrain (US bases hit after the
US struck Qeshm island); Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin meet in Shenzhen
ahead of the trilateral summit with Trump; Democrat Mary Peltola wins the
Alaska Senate seat, giving Democrats a projected 56-44 majority; Ukraine
hits the Kerch Bridge for a second day and part of its road span falls
into the strait; the Ebola outbreak in DR Congo more than doubles to over
11,000 cases and 5,400 deaths; Category 5 Cyclone Xavier hits the
Northern Cook Islands.

## How it works

`entry.png` (1024x1024) is the finished image, drawn by `render.py` with
Pillow at build time — flat shapes only, no photos, no external assets, no
network. `main.py` is standard library only: it just copies `entry.png`
to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`, so both grader runs write
byte-identical output.
